Where should ACE Dialer find phone numbers?

On the sites you add here, phone numbers become clickable — one click opens the number in ACE Dialer, ready for you to press Call. Everywhere else, this extension does nothing and cannot read the page.

    Adding a site asks Chrome for permission to read pages on that site — that access is what lets the extension find phone numbers. You can remove a site here at any time, which revokes it immediately.
