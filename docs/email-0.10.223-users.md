# Email draft — ACE Dialer 0.10.223 user announcement

**Subject:** ACE Dialer: text several favorites at once, and know when a scheduled text doesn't go out

---

Hi all,

Two additions to ACE Dialer are now live.

## 1. Send one message to several favorites

For when you want to touch base with a group of people and don't want to type the same text eleven times.

**Each person gets a normal one-to-one text in their own conversation.** This is not a group chat — nobody sees who else you messaged, and their replies come back to you privately in their own thread, exactly as they do today.

**You'll need a few favorites first.** Tap the star on any conversation, recent call, or voicemail to pin someone to your Favorites tab, or use the **+** button on that tab to add them by number. The send button only appears once you have more than one favorite.

**How it works** — open the **Favorites** tab and tap the **send icon** at the top, next to the **+**. Four steps:

1. **Pick who.** Tick as many contacts as you like, or **Select all**.
2. **Write the message.** You can start from one of your saved templates, or type it fresh. If your text uses **{firstName}**, each person's own name is filled in for them.
3. **Review.** You see every message exactly as that person will receive it, with their name already filled in. This is the point to catch anything wrong — once you send, it's gone.
4. **Send.**

A few things worth knowing:

- **A contact with several numbers gets one text**, on their primary number — not one per number.
- **Anyone who can't be messaged is named, with the reason.** A number you've blocked, someone no longer in your favorites, or a **{placeholder}** that couldn't be filled in for that person. Nobody is quietly skipped.
- **Messages go out at about one per second.** This is deliberate — sending a burst from one number gets messages filtered as spam by the phone carriers, and filtered messages disappear without telling anyone. So a list of 60 takes about a minute. You can close the window; it keeps sending.
- **A progress list** shows each person as their message is sent, then again once the carrier confirms it was delivered, so you can see exactly who received it.
- **The counter shows how many texts you'll actually be billed for** across everyone selected. Worth a glance: a single emoji, curly quote, or long dash cuts how much fits in one text, which can quietly double the count.
- Up to **200 people** in one send.

## 2. You'll be told when a scheduled text doesn't go out

Nothing to turn on for this one.

If you schedule a message and it fails to send, **ACE Bot now messages you on Teams** telling you who it was for, why it failed, and whether it's worth trying again.

Until now that failed quietly. The message would sit marked as failed and nothing told you — so you could believe a candidate had been contacted when nobody had. That was the gap worth closing.

Scheduled messages are also no longer given up on just because several were going out at the same moment, which previously could mark a perfectly good message as undeliverable.

## Getting the update

ACE Dialer updates itself. If you don't see the send icon on your Favorites tab, use **Check for updates** in the menu under your name, then restart the app when it offers to.

If you use ACE Dialer in a browser rather than the desktop app, refresh the page to pick it up.
