Hi all,

We have a new Chrome extension for ACE Dialer. It does two things:

  1. Phone numbers in JobDiva become clickable. Click one and it opens in the ACE Dialer app with the number already filled in. Nothing is dialled until you press Call, so a mis-click can’t call anyone.
  2. ACE Dialer in a side panel next to JobDiva. A keypad, your recent calls, your text conversations, your favorites, and a Follow-ups list of the missed calls, texts and voicemails you haven’t answered yet. The number on the extension’s icon tells you how many are waiting.

It takes about a minute to set up.

Install it

  1. In Chrome, open this link:
    Install ACE Dialer for Chrome
    (Using the link is quickest. Searching the store for it may not find it yet.)
  2. Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension.
    Chrome may say the extension isn’t trusted by Enhanced Safe Browsing yet. That’s normal for new extensions: click Continue to install.
  3. Pin it so it’s always visible: click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to ACE Dialer.

Using Microsoft Edge? Open the same link, click Allow extensions from other stores if Edge asks, then Add to Chrome.

JobDiva works straight away

Open JobDiva and refresh the page. Phone numbers now have a dotted underline. Click one and the ACE Dialer app comes forward with the number filled in. There’s nothing to set up for JobDiva.

The first time you click a number, Chrome asks “Open ACE Dialer?”. Tick Always allow and click Open ACE Dialer, and it won’t ask again.

Want it on another site too? Click the ACE Dialer icon, then the gear icon, type the site’s address and click Add site.

The side panel

Click the ACE Dialer icon, then Sign in with Microsoft with your usual ApTask account. Five tabs run along the bottom:

  • Keypad: type or paste a number. It shows whether the number is Mobile, Landline, VoIP or Toll-free, the same as the dialer’s keypad.
  • Recents: your calls, exactly as they appear in the dialer.
  • Messages: your text conversations, newest first, with new texts marked.
  • Favorites: the people you’ve starred in the dialer.
  • Follow-ups: missed calls, texts and voicemails from the last 14 days that you haven’t answered yet. A missed call drops off once you call or text that person back.

Every Call and Text button opens the number in ACE Dialer, ready for you to press Call or Send. Calls themselves still happen in the ACE Dialer app.

Good to know

  • The extension only reads JobDiva and any sites you add. It can’t see any other website, and it never touches a box you’re typing in.
  • The panel shows who and when, never what was said. To read a text or hear a voicemail, open the dialer.
  • The ACE Dialer app needs to be installed and signed in for clicks to work. If you already have the web dialer open in a Chrome tab, the number goes there instead, and a call you’re on in that tab isn’t interrupted.
  • Clicking a number does nothing? Check that the ACE Dialer app is running (look for its icon near the clock), then try again. If the number looked wrong, or two numbers were next to each other, the dialer says so rather than guessing.
